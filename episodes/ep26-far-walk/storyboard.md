# ep26 — How far will you walk in your lifetime?

Source: Althoff et al., Nature 2017 (Stanford, 717,527 phone users, world average 4,961 steps/day; via Live Science). Earth equator ≈ 40,075 km; Moon ≈ 384,400 km.
Maths: 4,961 × 0.7 m ≈ 3.5 km/day → ×365 ≈ 1,268 km/yr → ×80 ≈ 101,000 km ≈ 2.5 laps of Earth ≈ 0.26 of the way to the Moon. 2D only (no 3D today, CPU budget).
