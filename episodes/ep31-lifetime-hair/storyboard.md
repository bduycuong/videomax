# ep31 — How many hairs will you lose in your lifetime?

Facts: American Academy of Dermatology "Do you have hair loss or hair shedding?" (aad.org/hair-shedding): normal to shed 50–100 hairs a day; a dermatologist can tell shedding from hair loss. MedlinePlus "Hair loss" (medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003246.htm): scalp has about 100,000 hairs. Cleveland Clinic "Hair Follicle" (my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/23435-hair-follicle): growth phase 2–7 years, then rest/shedding, then the follicle grows a new hair.
Arithmetic (Python): 75 × 365 = 27,375/yr ("about twenty seven thousand"); × 80 = 2,190,000 ("about two million"); range 50/day → 1,460,000, 100/day → 2,920,000 ("one and a half to three million"); 2,190,000 / 100,000 = 21.9 ("about twenty times", range ~15–29). 2D only (CPU budget).
