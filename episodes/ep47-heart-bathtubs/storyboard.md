# ep47 — How many bathtubs of blood does your heart pump in one day?

Facts: Cleveland Clinic "Facts about the heart" (health.clevelandclinic.org/facts-about-the-heart): nearly 5.7 L (1.5 gal) per minute; about 100,000 beats a day;
about 96,000 km (60,000 mi) of vessels, could circle Earth at the equator twice; adult heart about the size of two clasped hands.
Calc: 5.7 × 1,440 = 8,208 L ≈ 8,200 L/day; ÷150 L bathtub = 54.7 ≈ 55; ×365.25×80 = 239.8 million L ≈ 240 million; ÷2.5 million L Olympic pool = 96 ("nearly 100").
