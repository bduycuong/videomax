# ep44 — If you could drive a car to the Sun, how long would it take?

Facts: NASA Science "Sun: Facts" — the Sun is about 93 million miles (150 million km) from Earth; NASA "Cosmic Distances" — 1 AU ≈ 150 million km, the Sun is about 8 light-minutes away.
Maths (Python): 149.6e6 km / 100 km/h = 1,496,000 h ≈ 1.5 million h = 62,333 days ≈ 170.7 years (spoken "about 170"). Jet 900 km/h → 18.96 ≈ 19 years. Light 149.6e6 / 299,792 = 499 s ≈ 8.3 min. 100 km/h ≈ 62 mph ("about 60").
