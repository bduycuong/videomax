# ep42 — How much does all the air on Earth weigh?

Facts: Trenberth & Smith 2005, J. Climate, "The Mass of the Atmosphere: a Constraint on Global Analyses" (NCAR): total mass 5.148e18 kg ≈ 5.15e15 t → "about five quadrillion tons" (metric). Sea-level pressure 101,325 Pa / 9.81 = 10,329 kg per m² ≈ 10 t. UCAR MetEd "The atmosphere in the vertical": half the mass below the 500 mb level ≈ 5.5 km. Standard atmosphere at 8,849 m: 314 hPa = 31% of sea-level → ≈ two thirds of the air below Everest's summit. Body skin ≈ 1.8 m² × 10.3 t ≈ 18.6 t → "about eighteen tons". Small car ≈ 1 t → "pile of ten small cars" (about).
