# ep38 — How many of your cells die and get replaced every single day?

Facts: Sender & Milo 2021, Nature Medicine "The distribution of cellular turnover in the human body" (reported by Scientific American and the Weizmann Institute): about 330 billion cells replaced per day, roughly 1% of the ~30 trillion cells; by number ~86–90% are blood cells, most of the rest gut lining cells (live < 1 week); red blood cells live up to ~120 days; total turnover mass a bit under 100 g/day; fat cells ~a decade, many muscle cells decades.
Arithmetic (Python): 330e9 / 86400 = 3.82 million per second ("about three point eight million"); x ~70 s = 267 million ("more than two hundred million" during this video); 330e9 / 30e12 = 1.1% ("about one percent"). Two eggs ~ 100 g ("roughly the weight of two eggs").
