# ep45 — Who has more bones, a newborn baby or you?

Facts: Cleveland Clinic (health.clevelandclinic.org/how-many-bones-does-a-baby-have): newborns 275–300 bones; my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/25176-bones: about 270 at birth, adults 206 (206–213 with variants);
fontanelles close within the first year or two. NCBI StatPearls "Sacral Vertebrae" (NBK551653): sacrum = 5 fused vertebrae, fusion begins at puberty, complete by 25–30.
Cleveland Clinic "facts about the skeletal system": 27 bones per hand + 26 per foot = 106 of 206 (51 %), babies' kneecaps are cartilage.
