# ep48 storyboard — Is a bolt of lightning hotter than the Sun?
1. hook — storm cloud + bolt ink themselves (ink), sun inks on the right, "vs", red "?" on "the Sun". → technique 10/curiosity gap.
2. guess — three sticky notes: cooler / same / WAY hotter pop on each option. → 14 curiosity gap.
3. intro — MAX WISE logo, small thermometer inks on "giant thermometer". → ink.
4. sun — three.js toon Sun spins (3D hero); right-edge thermometer fills to the 10,000°F mark on "ten thousand degrees". → 3 ruler that never leaves (thermometer).
5. bolt — same thermometer starts at the Sun mark; bolt inks out of the cloud, white flash + ZAP!, mercury and counter run 10,000→50,000°F. → 3 ruler + 10 onomatopoeia.
6. times — five numbered suns drop and stack beside one lightning bar of equal height; "50,000 ÷ 10,000 = 5", red 5× tag. → 2 countable units.
7. fast — ruler 0 to 1 s, highlighter band fills the first half, bolt flickers inside it, chip "less than ½ second". → 9 honest range / timeline.
8. thunder — cutaway of air around the bolt: air dots burst outward then shrink back, pressure rings ripple, BOOM!. → 1 simulate + 6 cutaway.
9. count — flash-to-bang track 0–15 s, sound wave rides along it (along) with a seconds counter, 1/2/3 mi marks every 5 s, "15 ÷ 5 = 3 miles". → 1 simulate + along.
10. outro — recap chip "Lightning ≈ 5× the Sun's surface", bolt > sun doodle, safety pill "thunder roars → go indoors", Stay curious!. → callback to hook.
