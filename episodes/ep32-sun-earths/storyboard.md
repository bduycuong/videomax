# ep32 — How many Earths could fit inside the Sun?

Facts: NASA Science "Sun: Facts" (science.nasa.gov/sun/facts): 1.3 million Earths fill the Sun's volume; more than 330,000 Earths to match its mass; 99.8 % of the solar system's mass. NASA NSSDC Sun Fact Sheet (nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/sunfact.html): mean radius 695,700 km vs Earth 6,371 km → ratio 109.2; volume ratio 1,304,000; mass ratio 332,900.
Arithmetic (Python): 695700/6371 = 109.2; 109² = 11,881 (≈ "almost twelve thousand"); 109³ = 1,295,029 ≈ 1.3 million (NSSDC 1,304,000). Door 2.0 m / 109.2 = 18 mm ≈ a nickel (21 mm). Packing: 1.3 M is volume (squished Earths); round balls leave gaps so fewer fit — said without a number (no reputable figure checked).
