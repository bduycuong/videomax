# ep40 — If you unrolled all the DNA in your body, how far would it reach?

Facts:
- Human genome ≈ 3 billion base pairs (NIH genome.gov, Human Genome Project FAQ); most cells are diploid → two copies ≈ 6.2 billion bp.
- Rise per base pair in B-DNA ≈ 0.34 nm (BioNumbers 100667, Langridge et al. 1960) → 6.2e9 × 0.34 nm ≈ 2.1 m per cell ("about two meters").
- Sender, Fuchs & Milo 2016, PLoS Biology "Revised estimates for the number of human and bacteria cells in the body": ≈ 30 trillion human cells (reference man); red blood cells ≈ 84%; "almost 90% … enucleated (26·10^12), mostly red blood cells and platelets", "≈ 3·10^12 nucleated cells".
- NASA Pluto facts: Pluto averages ≈ 5.9 billion km from the Sun (39 AU); 1 AU ≈ 150 million km.
Arithmetic (Python): 3e12 × 2 m = 6e12 m = 6 billion km; / 150 million km = 40 Earth–Sun distances; / 5.9 billion km = 1.02 → "about as far as Pluto is from the Sun". Popular version: 37e12 × 2 m = 74 billion km (shown only as the crossed-out myth, number of cells only).
