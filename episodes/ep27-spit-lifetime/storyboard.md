# ep27 — How much spit will you make in your lifetime?

Facts: Cleveland Clinic (salivary glands: about 1–2 L/day; amylase; protects teeth), Britannica (1–1.5 L/24 h; amylase), Cambridge MRCS guide (0.5–1 L/day). Script uses an honest range "half a litre to one and a half litres", typical ≈ 1 L.
Arithmetic: 1 L × 365 = 365 L/yr; /150 L tub = 2.4 tubs/yr; × 80 yr = 29,200 L ≈ 30,000 L ≈ 195 tubs ("around two hundred"); low end 0.5 L → 14,600 L ≈ 97 tubs ("about a hundred"). Olympic pool 50×25×2 m = 2,500,000 L. 2D only (no three.js).
