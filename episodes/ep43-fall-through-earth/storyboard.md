# ep43 — If you jumped into a hole through the Earth, how long would you fall?

Facts: Klotz 2015, "The gravity tunnel in a non-uniform Earth", American Journal of Physics 83, 231 (arXiv 1308.1342; phys.org 2015-03).
Uniform-density Earth (old textbook): 42 min 12 s end to end. Real density from seismic data (PREM): 38 min 11 s ≈ 38 min, so ≈ 19 min to the centre.
Inside Earth gravity rises to a maximum of about 1.09 g (≈ 9 % stronger) near the outer-core boundary, then falls roughly linearly to 0 at the centre.
Air drag ignored; without anyone catching you, you oscillate forever. Speed is greatest at the centre.
