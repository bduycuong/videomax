# ep37 — How long would your fingernails grow if you never cut them?

Facts: Yaemsiri et al. 2010, J Eur Acad Dermatol Venereol (PubMed 19744178): fingernails 3.47 mm/month, toenails 1.62 mm/month; little fingernail grew slower than the others; no significant left/right difference. Cleveland Clinic "Nail anatomy" (my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/nail-anatomy): fingernails about 3 mm/month, toenails about 1.5 mm/month.
Arithmetic (Python): 3.47 × 12 = 41.6 mm/yr ("about four centimeters"); 3.47 / 30.4 ≈ 0.114 mm/day ("about a tenth of a millimeter", ≈ one sheet of copy paper ≈ 0.1 mm); × 80 yr = 3,331 mm ("about three point three meters"); × 10 nails = 33.3 m ("about thirty three meters"; ~3.3 m per story → about ten stories); 1.62 / 3.47 = 0.47 ("about half as fast"). A normal room ceiling is about 2.4–2.7 m, so 3.3 m is taller. 2D only (no natural 3D hero).
