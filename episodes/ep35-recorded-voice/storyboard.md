# ep35 — Why does your voice sound so weird on a recording?

Facts: Scientific American, "Why does my voice sound so different when it is recorded and played back?" — your voice reaches the cochlea by two routes: air conduction (mouth -> air -> ear canal -> eardrum -> middle ear) and bone conduction (vocal-cord vibrations through the bones/tissue of the head straight to the cochlea). Bone conduction boosts the lower frequencies, so you hear yourself fuller and deeper; a microphone only picks up the air route, so playback sounds thinner/higher, and it is roughly what others hear. Plugging the ears and humming makes the bone-conducted sound dominate (louder, boomier). Qualitative only, no Hz/dB numbers. 2D only (2-CPU box shared with another build).
