# ep39 — How many times does lightning flash on Earth every second?

Facts: Christian et al., NASA Optical Transient Detector global lightning (NTRS 20020051098): about 44 ± 5 flashes per second worldwide, ~1.4 billion per year, land:ocean ≈ 10:1. US National Weather Service "Introduction to Lightning": old 100 per second figure is a 1925 estimate; up to ~2,000 thunderstorms in progress at any moment. NWS "How hot is lightning?": ~50,000 °F, about five times the Sun's surface.
Arithmetic (Python): 44 x 60 = 2,640 per minute; 44 x 86,400 x 365 = 1.388e9 ≈ 1.4 billion per year.
3D: hook only (toon Earth turning, yellow flashes popping on the continents).
